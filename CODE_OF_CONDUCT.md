# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility when you get something wrong, and moving on
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it
- Being patient with people who are new to the codebase, the tooling, or the
  language they are writing in

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or sustained disruption
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and code review — and whenever you are representing the
project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer at santoshshinde2012@gmail.com. Reports are
handled privately, and the reporter's identity is not disclosed.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not meet this
standard, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they
judge inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Enforcement is proportionate: a
private word first where that is likely to work, a ban where it is not.
